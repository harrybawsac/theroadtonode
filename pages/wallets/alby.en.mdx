# Alby Hub

<Callout emoji="&#x2139;">
        Time: 5 minutes
</Callout>

[Alby Hub](https://albyhub.com/) is a powerful self-custodial lightning wallet with the easiest to use lightning network node that lets you take full control of your bitcoin.
![Alby Hub Overview](/albyhub-overview.png)

## Installation
Alby Hub is available on Alby Cloud, other cloud options, for Windows, macOS and Linux servers. Choose your preferred option [here](/https://guides.getalby.com/user-guide/alby-account-and-browser-extension/alby-hub/alby-hub-flavors)

## Setup
Alby Hub provides an onboarding list that makes the setup quick and easy.
![Alby Hub Setup](/albyhub-setup.png)

## Connect to apps
Leverage the full potential of Alby Hub as your center of bitcoin payments with the integrated App Store. Connect it to the Alby Browser Extension for payments in your browser, to Alby Go for payments on the go and to many other apps.
![Alby Hub App Store](/albyhub-appstore.png)
